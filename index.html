A personal perspective

Just as a model learns to generalize from diverse data, I want to grow through the full range of human experience. Let me live more, face the many questions life puts before me, and know both joy and struggle. Life is both a cage and a playground. I want to draw closer to the truth of existence and become more fully myself—even if none of it has any ultimate meaning, even if it all comes to nothing in the end.
